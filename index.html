Built honestly

  • The stemmer only accepts a cut it can confirm in its 10,503-word lexicon, so words missing from it, like tallaal (vaccine), stay unmerged.
  • Irregular plurals such as guryaha (the houses) need hand-written overrides.
  • The corpus comes from the SOPC-v2 dataset; whether its texts are human-written is still being checked.
  • The evaluation uses 10 queries judged by one non-Somali speaker through translation: indicative, not conclusive.

          For Somali speakers. A stemmer groups different forms of a word together so that a search for one form also finds the others (for example dugsi, dugsiga, dugsiyada). Please check its groups: mark any word that is not a form of the same basic word as the others. Plurals, "the", possessives and verb forms belong together; different words that only look alike do not.
